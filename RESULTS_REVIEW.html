The disagreement audit — the Matters Arising

done · CQ500

Among Path-B's prose-silent misses (finding present, triage-urgent, report never mentions it; n=230, 33% of all Path-B false negatives):

median P(true dx) = 0.90

vs 0.21 when the finding is truly absent · separation AUROC 0.86 (95% CI 0.84–0.88) · overall 0.94 (0.93–0.95)

Reading: when the prose goes silent, the diagnostic head is still ~90% confident the finding is there. The encoder perceived it; the prose path lost it — a direct, quantitative counter to the "perception failure" attribution, and the case for rendering findings from Path A. This is the MA's headline (Figure 1b in the repo).

Per-case the effect is sharp — one occult fracture went P 0.01 → 0.80 on the bone-kernel series. Multi-series input helps bone findings and degrades nothing else.
